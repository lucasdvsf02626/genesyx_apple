## 1. Onboarding (pre-auth flow)

State machine: **Splash → Intro → Quiz → Readiness Summary → (Waitlist) → Auth → app.** Every exit routes through Auth; only a successful sign-in unlocks the tabs. Background `GenesyxColor.background`.

### 1.1 Splash
**Use:** brand landing; new user starts quiz, returning user signs in. Four decorative `BrandOrb`s.
- Wordmark **"GENESYX"** (`gxTitle`, `foreground`) · Eyebrow **"Step into the future of fertility"** (`primary`).
- Headline: **"Feel informed, supported and ready for your conception journey."**
- Body: **"A premium, gently-guided companion blending cycle awareness, nutrition and supplement support."**
- Disclaimer (✨ `sparkles`, `primary`): **"Educational wellness support — not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns."**
- **Buttons:** "Start Your Personalised Quiz" (→) — `GxPrimaryButton` **primary** → Intro. · "Sign in" — `GxGhostButton` (`foreground@80%`) → Auth.

### 1.2 Intro
**Use:** three-benefit value pitch before the quiz.
- Headline **"Your fertility preparation, gently guided"**; body **"Genesyx blends cycle awareness, nutrition, and supportive insights into one calm space."**
- Benefit cards (icon tile colour → title / desc):
  1. `heart` **electricLavender** — "Understand your cycle" / "Recognise patterns with calm, clear guidance."
  2. `leaf` **electricBlue** on powderBlue tile — "Support fertility nutrition" / "Cycle-aware food and supplement focus."
  3. `chart.bar` **electricPink** on powderPink tile — "Receive tailored insights" / "Gentle observations based on your tracking."
- **Buttons:** back chevron (`GxBackButton`) → Splash. · "Continue" — `GxPrimaryButton` **primary** → Quiz.

### 1.3 Quiz (5 questions)
**Use:** personalisation. Progress bar tinted **primary**, label "n/5". Options are `GxOptionPill` (selected = **primary**). Continue button `GxPrimaryButton` **primary**, disabled (`primary@45%`) until an option is chosen; label "See My Summary" on Q5 else "Continue". Some questions show a "Did you know?" alert (button "Continue") before advancing.

| Q | Question | Helper | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 stage | "Where are you in your conception journey?" | "There's no wrong answer — we'll tailor your experience." | Just starting to think about it · Actively preparing my body · Trying to conceive now · Looking for extra support |
| 2 cycle | "How regular does your cycle usually feel?" | "An honest answer helps us personalise your insights." | Very regular, predictable · Mostly regular with small shifts · Often irregular · I'm not sure yet |
| 3 supplements | "Are you currently taking fertility supplements?" | "We'll build a plan that fits where you are." | Yes, a full routine · A few key ones · Not yet · I'd love guidance on this |
| 4 gender | "When it comes to your baby's sex, what feels right for you?" | "This is just for you — we keep it gentle and private." | I have a hope in mind · I'm happy either way · I'd rather not say |
| 5 support | "What would you like the most support with?" | "Choose what feels most important right now." | Fertility nutrition guidance · Understanding my cycle · Supplement support · Feeling calm and informed |

**Q2 fact — "Did you know?":** "Only about 13% of cycles are exactly 28 days. A healthy cycle can range from 21 to 35 days — your rhythm is uniquely yours, and tracking it reveals your most fertile window." (Q4's old diet/sex-selection "fact" was deliberately removed as unsupported.)

### 1.4 Readiness Summary
**Use:** post-quiz reassurance + next steps, offering the free guide. One `BrandOrb`.
- Eyebrow pill **"Your readiness summary"** (`primary`) · Title **"A thoughtful starting point"** · Body "You're already taking meaningful steps. Here's where Genesyx will support you next."
- Insights card (icon `primary`): "Cycle awareness" → "Build a steady tracking rhythm" · "Nutrition focus" → "Folate, omega-3, and zinc-rich foods" · "Daily support" → "Gentle prompts and supplement plan".
- Next-steps card (bg powderBlue@18%, ✓ `primary`): "Start logging your cycle for 7 days" · "Review your personalised nutrition focus" · "Save the free fertility nutrition guide".
- **Buttons:** "Unlock My Free Guide" (`book`) — `GxPrimaryButton` **primary** → Waitlist. · "Register / Login to continue" — `GxGhostButton` → Auth.

### 1.5 Waitlist
**Use:** email capture for the free guide. ⚠️ **Currently cosmetic — validates format and shows success, but does not send/store the email anywhere** (no Supabase call).
- Eyebrow **"Free with early access"** (`primary`) · Title **"A gentle guide to fertility nutrition"** · Body "Sent straight to your inbox when you join the Genesyx waiting list."
- Email field: placeholder "your@email.com"; border `border`, turns **`destructive`** on error. Error string: **"Please enter a valid email address."** (regex `^[^@\s]+@[^@\s]+\.[^@\s]+$`).
- **Buttons:** "Join the Waiting List" — `GxPrimaryButton` **primary** (valid → success state). · "Register / Login to continue" — `GxGhostButton`.
- **Submitted state:** white ✓ in a 64px **primary** circle · Title **"You're on the list"** · Body "We'll send your free fertility nutrition guide to {email} shortly." · Button "Register / Login to continue" — `GxPrimaryButton` **primary**.

### 1.6 Auth
**Use:** the sign-in gate. Email+password (toggle sign-in/create), Sign in with Apple, Continue with Google. Content capped 360px wide.
- Wordmark "GENESYX". Title: create = **"Create your account"** / sign-in = **"Welcome back"**. Subtitle: create = "Save your cycle, nutrition, and partner info securely." / sign-in = "Sign in to sync your journey across devices."
- Fields: Name (create only, placeholder "Your name"), Email ("you@example.com"), Password (secure). Inputs h52 bg `card` border `border`.
- Validation errors: "Enter a valid email" · "Password must be at least 8 characters" · "Password is too long" · confirmation-required: "Almost there — check your inbox and confirm your email, then sign in." · generic: "Could not sign in. Please check your details and try again."
- Resend confirmation link (`primary`): success "Sent — check your inbox for the confirmation link." / fail "Couldn't resend just now. Please try again in a moment."
- **Buttons:** primary submit — plain button **bg primary** white text, h48 r12, label "Create account"/"Sign in"/"Please wait…". · "OR" divider. · **Sign in with Apple** — black `SignInWithAppleButton` h48. · **Continue with Google** — outlined (`border`) white bg h48. · Mode toggle link (`primary`) "Sign in"/"Create account". · "Back to app" — `GxGhostButton` (dismiss).
- Apple/Google errors: "Apple sign-in failed…", "Couldn't complete Apple sign-in…", "Couldn't complete Google sign-in. Please try again."; user cancels are silently ignored.

## 4. Vaginal pH tracker (`PhTrackerSection`, shown on Track & Nutrition)

**Use:** record vaginal pH; **scale 3.5–7.0**, two-band model. `PhStatus.classify`: **>4.5 = Elevated** (amber `phElevated`), **≤4.5 = Healthy** (green `phHealthy`). Legacy `urine` readings are never classified (shown neutral `mutedForeground`).

- Header: eyebrow "Track your pH" (`primary`) + title **"Vaginal pH Tracker"**. "Log pH" button = white text on **primary** capsule.
- **Caveat** (a11y `phCaveat`): "Vaginal pH naturally shifts across your cycle. Logging your cycle day alongside each reading helps you understand your own patterns."
- **Disclaimer** (a11y `phDisclaimer`, `PhCopy.disclaimer`): "This tracker is for your own record and isn't medical advice. If a reading worries you, or a pattern persists, please speak to a GP, nurse, or pharmacist."
- **Latest reading panel:** `drop.fill` in status colour; value "{x.x}"; chip = "HEALTHY"/"ELEVATED" (status colour@18%) or "urine (legacy)" for legacy.
- **Range selector:** 7d / 30d / 90d / All (default 30d); active pill `card` on `muted`.
- **Chart (`PhChart`):** domain **3.5–7.0**, Y marks 3.5/3.8/4.5/7.0; line+points **primary**; bands — Elevated (above 4.5) `phElevated`@10%, Healthy (3.8–4.5) `phHealthy`@12%, below 3.8 unshaded. Values clamped so legacy readings stay on-chart.
- **Empty:** "No readings yet" / "Log your first pH to start your chart." + `GxPrimaryButton` **primary** "Log pH".
- **One-time notice** (`ph_vaginal_notice_seen`, alert title "Vaginal pH tracking", button "Got it"): "This tracker now records vaginal pH. Your earlier readings are kept and marked 'urine (legacy)'. New readings are saved as vaginal pH, on a different scale."
- **Log/Edit sheet:** title "Log pH reading"/"Edit pH reading"; instruction "Track your vaginal pH from 3.5 to 7.0."; default value **4.2**; slider 3.5–7.0 step 0.1 tinted by status, ± buttons (`foreground` on `muted`); big value in status colour + HEALTHY/ELEVATED chip; "When" date picker (≤ now); notes (≤500 chars). "Save" (new → saved as `vaginal`); "Cancel" (new) or destructive "Delete" (edit).
- **Insights meaning (`PhInsightLogic`):** healthy → "Your recent readings sit within the typical healthy range."; elevated → "Your recent readings are above the typical healthy range." + signpost "If readings stay above the usual range over several days, a GP or pharmacist can talk it through with you." (recommendation shown only when elevated). Legacy urine readings excluded from all computation.

## 7. Insights tab

**Use:** deterministic summaries of the user's own data; honest empty states; never invents values. One week = current ISO week (Mon–Sun). Card radius 24. Order below.

- **Header:** "Your Insights" / "Understanding your patterns helps you make informed, empowered decisions for your wellbeing."
- **My logs link:** `book.closed.fill` **primary** tile; "My logs" / "See everything you've tracked" → Log history.
- **Consistency:** title + "This week" (`primary`). Tiles "Daily streak"/"Weekly streak". Week dots 7 circles **primary** (+white ✓) / `muted`. Insight line (never scolding) + optional "Best daily streak: {n} days". *Meaning: how consistently she logs.*
- **Vaginal pH** (a11y `phCaveat`): "Open tracker" (`primary`). Current value coloured by status + HEALTHY/ELEVATED chip; 7-day & 30-day avg tiles ("%.2f"/"—"); trend badge (↑/↓/→ "vs previous"); insight + recommendation + count line + the cycle caveat. Empty: "No pH readings yet. Log your first one on Track or Nutrition." *Meaning: two-band trend of vaginal-only readings.*
- **Hydration:** "This week" (**electricBlue**). 7 bars gradient **electricBlue→powderBlue** (logged) / flat `muted` (none), dashed **2.4 L goal** line. Tiles "7-day total {L}", "Days on goal {n}/7". Delta row (only when both weeks have data). *Meaning: weekly water vs goal + week-over-week.*
- **Nutrition consistency:** "This week" (**electricBlue**). Bars = supplements/day as fraction of plan. Tiles "Days logged {n}/7", "Supplements taken {total}".
