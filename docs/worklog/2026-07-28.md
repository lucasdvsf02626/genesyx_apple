### Non-reproducing edge notes (flagged, not fixed — surgical discipline)
- **#1 midnight race:** Home calls `cyclePhase` with the default `target: .today()` while Track/Nutrition/Insights capture `let today` at view init. Across a midnight re-render these could differ for one instant; self-heals next render. Trivial to harden (pass explicit `today` to Home) if desired — not a reproduced bug.
- **#3 LogView snapshot:** `LogView` seeds a local `@State waterMl` once (`populate()`); if left open while water changes elsewhere, "Save log" could last-writer-wins overwrite. Not reachable in normal modal flow (hydration edits are a separate modal). Not the Android divergence.
- **#5 `readings.last`:** "latest" is by sort order, not `recordedAt`; a back-dated reading may not be the newest timestamp. Sort semantics, not a freshness bug.
